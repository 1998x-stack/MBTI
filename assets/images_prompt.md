
# 🧠 MBTI Animal Logo Prompts (Markdown Edition)

Each MBTI type is matched with an animal and described through two unique logo prompts (tone, simplicity, wording, and design goal).

---

## ENTJ – 霸王龙 🦖

### 🔥 Prompt 1

* **Tone**: Bold and Dominant
* **Simplicity**: Minimal
* **Words**: Yes ("ENTJ")
* **Prompt**:

  > Design a bold minimalist logo featuring a stylized T-Rex silhouette and the word 'ENTJ'. The design should reflect authority, leadership, and decisiveness. Use strong, angular shapes and dark neutral colors like black or dark red for impact.

### 🔥 Prompt 2

* **Tone**: Strategic and Commanding
* **Simplicity**: Moderate
* **Words**: No
* **Prompt**:

  > Create a logo that features a moderately detailed T-Rex head roaring, with visual lines or rays symbolizing leadership and influence. Use a sharp, strategic visual style. Avoid text; focus on the iconic representation.
